OUR PROCESS

From rider idea to a showroom masterpiece.

01

Consultation & Intake

Discuss the bike, color palette, reference concepts, and budget.

02

Design Concept & Samples

Spray test swatches and choose exact pearls, flakes, and graphics.

03

Surface Stripping & Epoxy Priming

Sand to base, neutralize metal/plastics, and seal with anti-rust epoxy.

04

Custom Painting & Airbrush Art

Apply multi-stage base, candy dye layers, hand-masking, and artwork.

05

Clearcoat, Mirror Buffing & Handover

Ultra-high solids 2K clear baked under IR lamps, detailed, and delivered.
